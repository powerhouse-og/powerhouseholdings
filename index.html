04 How we operate

Built like infrastructure,
run like a craft.

The system proposes; a human settles it; the system gets permanently better.

Operating principle — the Powerhouse learning loop
a.

Contracts, not tables

Modules share versioned interfaces, never storage. Boundaries are verified at boot, so a missing piece fails loudly at startup — not quietly at 2 a.m.

b.

Privacy as architecture

Local-first where possible, consent-gated at the source where not. Nothing is telemetered that doesn't need to be; the user's data stays the user's.

c.

Humans hold authority

Machine recognition drafts; people with standing confirm. Nothing consequential leaves any of our systems without a human's approval.

d.

Run for the long term

No dependency sprawl, no build-step archaeology. Systems designed so one part can be honed for years without rebuilding the rest.
