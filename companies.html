Powerhouse Digital.

RoleWeb design, build & digital marketing
OwnershipWholly owned
ServesBusinesses of every type and size
Websitepowerhousedigital.net →

Custom, secure websites with whatever functionality a business needs — plus the marketing and brand design to match.

Most small-business websites are templates wearing a logo. Powerhouse Digital builds the other kind: designed around the specific business, engineered in-house, and delivered fast, secure, and ready to be maintained for years rather than rebuilt in two.

Functionality means whatever the business actually runs on — lead capture, booking, quoting, e-commerce, payments, customer portals — built into the site rather than bolted alongside it. And because a site is only as good as what drives people to it, the company carries the rest of the digital presence too: brand and identity work, search visibility, and the marketing that turns a website into a storefront.

    Custom-designed, hand-built sites — no page builders, no template debt — engineered for speed and security.

  • PD·02

    Booking, quoting, lead capture, payments, portals: whatever the business needs its website to do, built in.

  • PD·03

  • PD·04

    Search visibility, campaigns, and content that bring the right customers to the door.

  • PD·05
    E-commerce

  • PD·06
    Hosting & care

    Fast, secure hosting with the site looked after continuously — updates, backups, and a human who answers.

Powerhouse Consulting.

RoleGeneral business consultancy
OwnershipWholly owned
ExpertiseLogistics · systems · vertical integration · developing markets
EngageThrough Powerhouse Holdings →

General business consultants with deep expertise in logistics, systems, vertical integration, and developing markets.

Powerhouse Consulting works on the machinery of a business rather than its slideware: how goods move, how records keep up with them, which steps belong in-house, and where the next market actually is. The practice has designed and operated logistics networks end to end, built the systems working operations run on, and guided organisations from government contractors to working farms.

Engagements are confidential by default — much of the practice's best work is under NDA and will stay there. Clients keep the credit; the results show up in their numbers.

  • PC·01
    Logistics & operations

    Intake, audit, reconciliation, shipping, returns — designed as one chain of custody, then proven in operation.

  • PC·02
    Systems design

    The record-keeping a business actually runs on, built so the ledger keeps up with the loading dock.

  • PC·03
    Vertical integration

    Which steps to bring in-house, in what order, and how to run them once they're yours.

  • PC·04
    Developing markets

    Entering markets that are still forming — where the playbook doesn't exist yet and judgment has to stand in for it.

  • PC·05
    Procurement & public work

    Finding, qualifying for, and winning contracted work — federal, state, and local included.

  • PC·06
    Discretion

    Confidential by default. References available where agreements allow.

A venture holding company — where longer-horizon ideas are built, held, and grown without a clock running on them.

Viresco — Latin, "to grow green, to flourish." Where the operating companies serve clients today, Viresco Ventures carries the portfolio's early-stage work: ventures that need years of patient building before they're ready to stand on their own. It gives that work a home with the structure of a holding company and none of the urgency of a fund.

Ventures are announced when they're ready, not before.
