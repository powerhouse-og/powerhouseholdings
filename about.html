Powerhouse Holdings is the parent behind Powerhouse Digital, Powerhouse Consulting, and the portfolio's venture holdings — the standard they are built to, and the discipline they are run with.

01 Who we are

Holding company by structure. Builder by nature.

Powerhouse Holdings, LLC is the company that owns our companies. It sells nothing itself — no services, no products, no hours. Its work is quieter and harder: deciding what deserves to exist, building it properly, and holding it to a standard once it does.

Where a traditional holding company allocates capital across businesses it acquires, Powerhouse allocates something scarcer — conviction — across businesses it creates. Powerhouse Digital and Powerhouse Consulting were not bought on a thesis; each was built inside the house around work that had already been proven, then given its own name, its own focus, and the parent's full backing.

The structure exists for the clients' sake as much as ours. A web company that only builds for the web and a consultancy that only consults stay sharp in a way a do-everything brand never does — and behind both stands a parent whose only product is the standard they share.

02 The operating model

How a company moves
through the house.

Four stages, applied identically to every company in the portfolio — whatever the field.

/ 01

Observe

Every company starts with work the house has already done well and a market that plainly needs it done properly. The craft comes first; the company is how it scales. Nothing gets a name until it has earned one.

Craft before company
/ 02

Build

The company is stood up inside the house: its offer defined, its brand drawn from the same guidelines as the parent, its operations designed before its first client. By launch day it behaves like a firm years older than it is.

Stood up, not spun up
/ 03

Operate

Each company runs under its own name with the parent's standard running through it — the same engineering culture, the same measured voice, the same bar for client work. The parent stays close enough to keep that true and far enough to let the company be itself.

Its own name, one standard
/ 04

Hold

There is no exit calendar. Companies are held permanently, which changes every decision inside them — toward maintainable work, durable client relationships, and promises the house intends to keep for decades. What one company learns, the house keeps for all of them.

Permanent capital

03 The standard

What a Powerhouse company means.

Craft

  • — Work designed for the specific client, never from a template
  • — Built to be maintained for years, not rebuilt in two
  • — Dependencies and middlemen earn their place or stay out
  • — Every promise written down, every deadline treated as one

Clients

  • — The smallest engagement gets the same bar as the largest
  • — Confidential by default; the client keeps the credit
  • — Plain answers, measured claims, no urgency theatre
  • — A human who answers, at every company, every time

Judgment

  • — Decisions made by the people accountable for them
  • — Nothing consequential leaves the house on autopilot
  • — Built to hold, so shortcuts have nowhere to hide
  • — When uncertain, the answer is the careful one

Leadership

Founder-led,
founder-run.

Powerhouse Holdings was founded and is led by Matthew Martin, who remains directly involved across every company in the portfolio. The house is deliberately small: decisions travel at the speed of conviction, and the person accountable for a company's promises is the person who set its standard.

That structure is a feature clients and partners feel directly — there is no layer between the standard and the person who holds it.
